Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect to the gigabit member WiFi?

Connect to the WorkspaceHub-Member-Secure network using your registered account email and your 4-digit check-in access PIN.

Can I bring visitors to my meeting room booking?

Yes! Up to your meeting room's capacity. Check in at the front desk concierge to receive temporary visitor lanyards.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

Any reservation cancelled at least 2 hours before the start time is automatically refunded or credited to your balance.
